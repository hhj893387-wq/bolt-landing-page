8. Privacy and usage statistics

(a) Stored on your device. Your downloaded files, download history, settings, cache, and logs are stored locally on your device in Bolt’s data folder. We do not receive them.

(b) Usage statistics. Bolt can send limited usage statistics and crash locations to a server we control (hosted by Supabase) to help us improve the software. [CHOOSE ONE: This is ON by default / This is OFF by default.] Each report contains:

  • a random install ID created on your device, and a random session ID created each time Bolt starts. These are not linked to your name or an account, but they let us recognize reports from the same installation;
  • the type of event (for example: app started or closed, link checked, download started, completed, failed, or cancelled, update clicked, a setting changed);
  • the Bolt version, your operating system name and version, and the time of the event;
  • short details about the event, such as the chosen format, quality or conversion type, whether playlist, clip, subtitle, or rename features were used, which website was used (the site name or domain), how long a download took, and a general error category;
  • for crashes only: the error type and the name of the function and program file, and the line number, where it happened. The error message itself is never sent.

(c) What we do not collect. We do not collect your name, email address, or any account details. We do not collect the links (URLs) you enter, video titles, file names, or the content of anything you download. We do not record or store your IP address as part of these statistics. As with any internet connection, the server receiving the statistics can technically see the IP address of the connection, and its hosting provider may log it under its own policies. We do not use it.

(d) Encryption. The details of each event are encrypted on your device before they are sent, using a public key built into Bolt. Only we hold the key needed to read them.

(e) Your choice. You can turn usage statistics off at any time in Bolt’s settings. When turned off, Bolt stops recording and sending statistics and deletes any not yet sent.

(f) Use and retention. We use the statistics only to understand how Bolt is used and to fix problems. We do not sell them or use them for advertising.
